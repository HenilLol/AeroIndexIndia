# Licensed Alternatives for Live Airfare and Route Tracking

This assessment excludes consumer-site scraping. It considers only provider-sanctioned commercial APIs and direct airline integrations that can be used with explicit account approval and server-side credentials.

| Alternative | Fare-price capability | Route or operational capability | Access finding | Initial fit for AeroIndex |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Amadeus Enterprise APIs | Potentially supports air offers through its enterprise portfolio. | Airline-focused enterprise APIs are available. | Amadeus states that its former self-service developer portal has been decommissioned and directs users to request access to Enterprise APIs. | Strong long-term distribution candidate, but requires enterprise onboarding and a commercial agreement. |
| Duffel API | The official API reference includes a Flights product area; product coverage must be validated in the account. | Flight shopping and operational fields depend on the selected Duffel product and supplier coverage. | Duffel provides developer documentation with account creation and sign-in entry points. | Viable evaluation candidate for a credentialed offer-search adapter, subject to India domestic supplier coverage and commercial approval. |
| Direct airline NDC API | Airline-controlled offer and ancillary pricing, subject to airline scope. | Airline schedule and shopping capabilities vary by airline. | Requires an approved commercial or technical relationship with the airline. | Best for a single-carrier benchmark where access is granted; not a broad market monitor alone. |
| Flight-status or schedule data provider | Does not generally supply booking fares. | Can augment route status, schedule changes, flight identity, and timing enrichment. | Must be separately licensed and should be paired with a fare source. | Useful secondary source for operational route context, not a replacement for airfare data. |
| Licensed travel-distribution API | Offer and price search depend on provider commercial coverage. | Often includes route, carrier, and availability context. | Requires account approval, credentialed access, and product-rights confirmation. | Suitable if its India domestic route and fare coverage meets the index methodology. |
| OAG Flight Info API | Does not position itself as a booking-fare source. | Provides schedules, real-time status, connections, seats, and reference data; its developer portal shows `Subscription-Key` authentication. | Offers a developer onboarding path and API key creation; production rights and coverage should be confirmed commercially. | Recommended companion for live route, schedule, and disruption intelligence alongside a separately licensed fare feed. |
| Cirium FlightStats Schedules API | Does not provide booking fares in the reviewed schedule reference. | Provides schedule lookups by airport, flight, and route, with Flight Status referenced as a companion service. | The route and airport schedule products are documented as Contract-plan offerings and support `appId`/`appKey` authentication options. | Strong enterprise alternative for route and schedule enrichment, but requires contract-plan licensing for the relevant route search capability. |
| Aviation Edge Flight Search API for India | The reviewed India-focused product page describes flight tracking, schedules, and airline routes; it does not document booking-fare offer responses. | Lists IATA flight and airline numbers, departure/arrival airport code filters, live status, schedules, and airline-route data for Indian flights. | Describes premium subscriptions and an API-key onboarding path. | Most concrete documented India-specific option for live route and airline operational tracking; use only as a route-status companion, not as the AeroIndex fare-price source. |

## Recommendation

Use **Amadeus Enterprise APIs or a direct airline NDC partnership** for a credentialed production fare source. Pair either with **Aviation Edge, OAG, or Cirium** when operational route, schedule, and status tracking is also required. Aviation Edge has the clearest reviewed India-specific route-tracking material, but it is not a documented fare source. Preserve AeroIndex’s validated historical observations as the public fallback whenever a live source is unavailable or ineligible for the requested route.

| Source role | Primary candidate | Why | Activation requirement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fare observations for the index | Amadeus Enterprise API or direct airline NDC | Licensed offer/pricing path suitable for market-price observations when contracted. | Signed commercial access, credentials, and confirmation of India domestic fare coverage. |
| Live routes, schedules, and status | Aviation Edge Flight Search API for India | Explicit India-focused route, airport, airline, schedule, and live-status material. | Premium API key and test confirmation for the required Indian airports and carriers. |
| Enterprise-scale route enrichment | OAG or Cirium | Strong schedule/status model, but commercial product rights apply. | Enterprise/contract access for the needed route, airport, or status products. |

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Amadeus Enterprise — fare source | **Unconfirmed** for the target routes; validate in sandbox or commercial discovery. | Offer/pricing portfolio available subject to contract. | Depends on the licensed air product. | Enterprise access request and issued credentials. | Medium to high. | Strong: ingest normalized offers, retain historical observations on failure. | Primary fare-source candidate after India coverage is confirmed. |

| Aviation Edge — route/status source | **Documented India-focused route and tracking material.** Validate target airports/carriers in a paid test. | No documented booking-fare capability in reviewed material. | Live status, schedules, airline routes, and airport/carrier filters. | Premium API key. | Low to medium. | Excellent: affects only route enrichment, never replaces price history. | Preferred initial operational route-status companion. |
| OAG — route/status source | **Unconfirmed** for named AeroIndex domestic routes; official material claims broad global schedules/status. | Not a reviewed fare source. | Schedules, real-time status, connections, seats, and master data. | Subscription key and commercial product selection. | Medium. | Excellent: independent enrichment feed. | Enterprise alternative where broader operations coverage is needed. |
| Cirium FlightStats — route/status source | **Unconfirmed** for named AeroIndex domestic routes; validate during contract evaluation. | Not a reviewed fare source. | Route, airport, flight schedules, plus companion status products. | Contract plan for route/airport schedules; `appId`/`appKey` options. | Medium to high. | Excellent: independent enrichment feed. | Enterprise alternative for route-level schedule analytics. |

> **Decision rule:** do not designate a production provider based on marketing coverage claims. Confirm at least the AeroIndex target airports, domestic routes, operating carriers, response fields, permitted use, rate limits, and production credentials in the provider’s sanctioned trial or contract environment before activation.
